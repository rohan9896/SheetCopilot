- (decision) Create an MVP PRD for a design-partner pilot of SheetCopilot as a web SaaS.
- (decision) Primary operator is a workshop engineer; workshop owner is buyer and organization administrator.
- (decision) MVP accepts single-page vector PDFs and clean scans with one primary flat part per drawing.
- (decision) Canonical output is a versioned parametric Part Definition JSON plus deterministic DXF; CadQuery and Fusion are deferred.
- (decision) Manufacturing-critical unknowns block approval; secondary operations such as countersinks may permit cut-profile export only after explicit acknowledgment.
- (decision) Nesting supports mixed approved part revisions and quantities when material and thickness are compatible.
- (decision) Nesting uses saved stock presets with job-level edits and editable placements; live inventory is deferred.
- (decision) MVP exports CAM-ready nominal-geometry DXFs and manifests, not machine-specific G-code or postprocessed toolpaths.
- (decision) Approval and export are permission-based, but the pilot does not require a separate four-eyes approver.
- (decision) Pilot acceptance requires independent DXF validation and successful import into one design-partner-nominated CAM application.
- (assumption) Vision-model providers remain replaceable and are selected through evaluation rather than hard-coded in the product contract.
- (event) Initial SheetCopilot MVP PRD draft created from the approved planning decisions and sample drawing.
